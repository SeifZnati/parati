To My Karina 💕

I'm writing this while you're not here, you're in Barcelona right now, enjoying yourself, and I hope you are having a good time. But I haven't spent a single hour without thinking about you. I miss you so much.

And yet, today is also about something bigger. Today is the day you came into this world and made everything around you brighter. Kari, I am so grateful that I got to meet you in this life, and if I didn't I would have looked for you everywhere in all my lives, because every moment I've spent talking to you, being with you, has been the best time of my life.

I really love you a lot. I appreciate everything you do. And I promise that I will always be here with you. I love just watching you study because you are the smartest person ever. I love hearing about your day. I love being with you at all times, just feeling your heart, listening to how it goes.

You are the best person I ever got to meet, and I am so grateful for that. Te amo, Kari. I can't even find the words to express it fully.

I want to see you become the person you dream of being, because that's the person I dream of standing next to, every single day of my life.

Happiest of birthdays. Feliz cumpleaños. I will always be next to you and I will always be richer in life, as long as you stay close to me.

Loving you is the best thing I have ever done in my life.

Thank you for existing. I am here for you, always and forever.

Forever yours,
Seif ❤️
