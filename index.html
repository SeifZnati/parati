To My Kari 💜

Dear Kari,

I am writing this the second night of the conference and I am not sure if I'm gonna be able to write everything on paper, so for now I will keep it like this.

I wanted to tell you, Kari, that I am not the best person ever. I make you sad and I cry and make tantrums because I'm like that and I want attention, and you know that I want attention because I applied to be MCP

But still, the main thing that kept me going in this organization and helped me want to be better than ever this year is being with you.

Seeing you, the supportive, hardworking person I get to be with, I have the chance to see you every day and listen to you be the smartest in the room.

I have always wondered why you loved me, because you are the person that makes everything make sense in my life. I don't even know how to do without you anymore.

AIESEC-related, I would say that you are the strongest person ever. Doing this thing twice : being MCVP twice, I couldn't dream of doing that, let alone in the same department in the same entity, and always bringing something new and making things happen.

You did this while managing studies, while managing yourself, and managing a huge ass baby like me — and I can't even understand how this is possible.

You're an inspiration for me in how you keep being resilient, how you can keep being a good person when everyone is being shitty to you. How you can continue bringing yourself to do things when everything is going bad. I admire you and you have no idea.

I wish I was even a bit like you, because I would be better, AIESEC would be better, and the world would be better.

Karina Vianey Prado Oropeza I love you, I love you, I love you, I love you.

And you always ask me why. Well, I will give you the reasons. The true reasons why I want to be with you until I die. (It rhymes a bit, jajajaj 🥹)

Reasons Why I Love You 💜

I will love you forever because you make me feel at home.

I will love you forever because you have seen me at my lowest and are still here for me.

I will love you forever because your happiness is the most important thing in my life.

I will love you forever because the list I wrote to you can be 10x bigger if I could do it.

I will love you forever because the distance from the moon to the earth keeps getting more every day. (Real Fact 🌙)

I will love you forever because I have never been sure I loved someone before I met you.

I will love you forever because I knew I loved you the day I knew you liked me.

I will love you forever because every time I look at you I fall in love again.

I will love you forever because every time I talk to you I fall in love again.

I will love you forever because I will never get bored of you at any moment in life.

I will love you forever because the version of my life without you, I cannot even imagine it.

I will love you forever because I can imagine my kids speaking in Spanish and French. 🇲🇽🇫🇷

I will love you forever because I want to travel the world with you and love you on every continent.

I will love you forever because the reasons I told you are just what the brain and eyes see, but the heart knows more than I can say.

Forever yours,
Seif 💜

I'm writing this while you're not here, you're in Barcelona right now, enjoying yourself, and I hope you are having a good time. But I haven't spent a single hour without thinking about you. I miss you so much.

And yet, today is also about something bigger. Today is the day you came into this world and made everything around you brighter. Kari, I am so grateful that I got to meet you in this life, and if I didn't I would have looked for you everywhere in all my lives, because every moment I've spent talking to you, being with you, has been the best time of my life.

I really love you a lot. I appreciate everything you do. And I promise that I will always be here with you. I love just watching you study because you are the smartest person ever. I love hearing about your day. I love being with you at all times, just feeling your heart, listening to how it goes.

Loving you is the best thing I have ever done in my life.

Thank you for existing. I am here for you, always and forever.

Forever yours,
